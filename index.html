About

After the success of last year, we will be running the University of Aberdeen GameJam again in 2027.

Teams have one week to develop a game from scratch on a competition theme, which is announced at the start of that week.

The week ends with an in-person event, where teams get feedback from the judges and present their games. The winners are announced that afternoon.

In-person event

The in-person event runs from 10:00 to 16:00 on Wednesday 3 February 2027.

  1. 01

    Get feedback

    Teams bring their games along and get feedback from the judges.

  2. 02

    Present your game

    Each team makes a short presentation of the game they built during the week.

  3. 03

    Winners announced

    The judges choose a winner for each prize and announce them that afternoon.

Prizes

Prizes will be announced closer to the time. Students in 1st and 2nd year will compete for separate prizes from students in 3rd year and above.

  • 1st and 2nd year students

    One set of prizes is for students in 1st and 2nd year.

  • 3rd year and above

    A separate set of prizes is for students in 3rd year and above.

Rules

  • Games must be developed from scratch during the week of the jam, between Wednesday 27 January and Wednesday 3 February 2027.
  • Games must follow the competition theme announced on Wednesday 27 January 2027.
  • Teams must sign up by Wednesday 20 January 2027 to take part.
  • Teams can have up to 4 members.
